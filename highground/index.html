Rank & fund

See exactly where the money runs out. Then fund more.

Put every initiative in the district’s own order. HighGround walks down the list, year by year and fund by fund, and draws the line where the money runs out.

  • Fill in around the gaps: fund smaller initiatives further down without delaying anything ranked higher past the year it’s needed.
  • Ways to fit more: one-move suggestions, like starting one project a year later, and what each would fund.
  • What would it take? Pick an initiative and a year; see the change that pays for it.
  • Year by year: when each initiative can actually be paid for, and what has to wait.
  • New hires and programs are checked against the General Fund forecast, every year they run.

Built for Iowa’s funds

SAVE, PPEL, V-PPEL and grants are tracked separately, year by year, so a project never quietly spends money a fund doesn’t have. A phase can split its cost between funds, or take whichever has room.

  • Existing debt, leases and ongoing spending (in dollars, or as a share of receipts) are taken out first.
  • The SF 2472 SAVE reduction, SAVE revenue-bond room and the 5% general-obligation limit.
  • Fund rules checked as you go, citing the Iowa Code, and a warning when a purchase may be counted twice.
  • Bonds, revenue bonds, leases and gifts, with what each scenario means for a home and an acre of farmland.
  • Scenarios side by side, so the board sees the trade-offs before it votes.

Built at a board table

HighGround began on a rural Iowa school board that needed a ten-year capital plan and one honest answer: what can we afford, when, and from which fund? The spreadsheet couldn’t say. The plan that came out of that work showed where the money really ran short, and where it didn’t.
