Funds and rules

Built on your state's funds and rules.

Each fund is tracked on its own, with its own rules, so a project is only ever paid from money that can legally pay for it. Available in Iowa and Nebraska, with more states coming one set of rules at a time.

  • Every capital fund year by year for the whole plan: balances, receipts, spending and debt
  • Existing debt, leases and ongoing spending come off the top, in dollars or as a share of receipts
  • Fund rules checked as you work, with the law cited, and a warning when money might be counted twice
  • Borrowing, leases and gifts planned alongside levies and grants
  • A five-year General Fund forecast with a likely range: solvency, unspent balance, spending authority and what a negotiated raise costs

Why we exist

Good decisions deserve better tools.

Willow Holler is an independent software company. We've watched good people on boards and councils, in districts of every size, try to make ten-year decisions with tools that couldn't show them the whole picture. They deserved better.

So we build software that starts from real problems, speaks plainly and respects that the decisions are yours. School districts first, with more states and more kinds of public bodies to come.
